About DriveNew

We exist to make Bahrain's roads safer - one confident driver at a time. Learn who we are, what we stand for, and the people behind your training.

Our story

From one yard to four branches across Bahrain

DriveNew began with a small team of instructors who believed learning to drive should be friendly, transparent and affordable. Today we train thousands of students every year across cars, motorcycles, heavy vehicles and forklifts - while keeping the personal attention that started it all.

🎯 Mission

Produce safe, skilled, responsible drivers through world-class training.

🔭 Vision

To be Bahrain's most trusted and innovative driving institute.

🤝 Values

Safety, integrity, patience, inclusion.

🌱 Commitment

Eco-driving and an electric-car fleet by 2028.

Milestones

Our journey

2010

Opened our first training yard in Sitra with 12 instructors.

2014

Launched heavy vehicle and forklift courses.

2017

Recognised by GDT for excellence in learner road safety.

2020

Introduced online registration, e-learning and the student app.

2023

Opened driving simulators and a ladies-only training wing.

2026

Fourth branch opened; first hybrid training fleet on the road.
