Pricing FAQ

Questions about fees

Are there hidden costs?

No. The only extras are GDT test fees and any additional hours if you need a retest.

Can I get a refund?

Unused lesson hours are refundable within 6 months minus registration fees, as per our refund policy.

Can I change from normal to shift later?

Yes — just pay the difference for the remaining hours.

Do offers combine with each other?

One discount per registration; we'll always apply the best one for you.
