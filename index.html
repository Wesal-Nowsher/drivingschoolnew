FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age for a car licence in the UAE?

17 years for a light vehicle (from 2025 rules), 17 for motorcycles, and 20 for heavy vehicles.

What documents do I need to register?

Emirates ID (or passport with visa), eye test result, 2 passport photos and, if applicable, your home-country licence.

Can I convert my foreign licence?

Holders from many countries can swap directly; others can take the "Golden Chance" evaluation test and skip most lessons.

How long does it take to get a licence?

Typically 4–8 weeks depending on your shift, lesson hours and RTA test availability. VIP Express can be faster.

What if I fail the road test?

You'll get extra lessons (minimum hours set by RTA) and rebook. Our VIP Platinum includes unlimited retests.

Do you offer automatic and manual cars?

Yes. Automatic is most popular; manual licences also allow you to drive automatics.
