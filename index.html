FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum age for a car licence in Bahrain?

17 years for a light vehicle (licence issued at 17 with conditions under current GDT rules), 18 for motorcycles, and 21 for heavy vehicles. Please confirm the latest requirements with GDT.

What documents do I need to register?

CPR card (or passport with valid residence permit), eye test result, 2 passport photos and, if applicable, your home-country licence.

Can I convert my foreign licence?

Holders from many countries (including GCC licences) can convert directly; others take a GDT evaluation test and may only need a short course.

How long does it take to get a licence?

Typically 4–8 weeks depending on your shift, lesson hours and GDT test availability. VIP Express can be faster.

What if I fail the road test?

You'll get extra lessons (minimum hours set by GDT) and rebook. Our VIP Platinum includes unlimited retests.

Do you offer automatic and manual cars?

Yes. Automatic is most popular; manual licences also allow you to drive automatics.
