Tips

Pass your GDT test first time

Check mirrors constantly

Examiners look for mirror checks every 5–8 seconds and before every manoeuvre.

Master the roundabout

Correct lane choice and signalling is the #1 reason students fail.

Stay calm & smooth

Gentle braking and steady speed show control. Take a breath before you start.
